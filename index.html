About

Hi, I'm Ryan Ly — a narrative scripter focused on taking pitches and turning them into gameplay sequences with branching outcomes, technical soundness, and strong player agency.

I've applied these skills directly in BG3's modding environment, scripting quests, reactive encounters, and branching dialogue for Path To Menzoberranzan, a custom campaign mod. Working within BG3's toolset gave me hands-on experience with the intersection of narrative design and scripting mechanics — implementing logic that responds to player choices and companion systems.

My D&D 5e modules reinforce the same fundamentals: structured narrative pitches, multi-outcome resolution systems, and encounter design that adapts to player decisions. Writing these as complete, playable documents required communicating complex branching logic clearly — the same skill needed to collaborate across narrative, art, and animation teams.

I'm passionate about RPGs, tabletop games, and building experiences where the player's choices genuinely matter. I'm eager to learn and master proprietary scripting frameworks, contribute to a collaborative team, and help craft the high-calibre games Larian is known for.
