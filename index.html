Projects

The Last Harvest of Willowbrook

Quest Design Sample • D&D 5e One-Shot • Levels 1–3

A narrative-driven one-shot where players must help an unprepared village prepare for a bandit attack. The module focuses on player choice and consequence — every major decision during the two-day preparation phase directly changes the difficulty and tone of the climactic boss fight inside the village mill.

Quest Design Highlights

  • Strong emphasis on preparation and consequence — choices in Act 2 meaningfully affect the final encounter
  • Memorable NPCs with personal stakes and emotional arcs
  • Climactic set-piece battle inside the mill against Captain Varga (difficulty scales based on player preparation)
  • Multiple satisfying endings based on how well the party rallied and prepared the villagers
