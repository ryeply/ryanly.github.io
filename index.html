About

Hi, I'm Ryan Ly. I'm a Game Designer focused on quest design, narrative systems, and player agency. I create experiences where choices feel meaningful and reactive to the player.

My work emphasizes building rich worlds, compelling characters, and systems that reward thoughtful decision-making. I've honed these skills through both video game development and tabletop RPG design, where I've created modules that put player preparation and consequence at the center of the experience.

I enjoy collaborating closely with writers, artists, and other developers to craft cohesive stories and gameplay that feel impactful.

Projects

The Last Harvest of Willowbrook

Quest Design • Tabletop RPG Module • 2026

A one-shot D&D 5e adventure for low-level characters. Players must rally an unprepared village and make meaningful preparation choices that directly impact the difficulty and outcome of a climactic boss fight inside the village mill. Designed to showcase player agency, consequence, and narrative-driven quest structure.

The Great Pie Heist

Quest Design • Tabletop RPG Module • 2025

A lighthearted D&D 5e one-shot adventure for levels 1-3. Players investigate the theft of fancy contest pies in the charming village of Honeybrook. They navigate quirky townsfolk, a skill challenge with toll-collecting squirrels, and confront a gang of pie-obsessed raccoon bandits in a dilapidated abandoned house. The flexible final confrontation supports multiple resolution paths: stealth heist, social negotiation, creative challenges, or silly combat. Designed to emphasize player choice, creative problem-solving, environmental interaction, as well as comedic storytelling through meaningful consequences.

Quest & Level Design • Baldur’s Gate 3 Custom Campaign Mod • 2025

Contributed to quest and level design on Path To Menzoberranzan, a custom campaign mod for Baldur’s Gate 3.
